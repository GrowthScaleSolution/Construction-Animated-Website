# Contact Page Redesign Brief

## 1. PAGE PERSONALITY
Premium construction enquiry command centre.

## 2. UNIQUE VISUAL DIRECTION
- compact functional hero
- clear quick-action contact dock
- map-led local section
- structured site-visit request experience
- premium form panels
- construction and architectural details
- no homepage-style video hero
- no About-style editorial storytelling layout
- no copied blueprint timeline

## 3. RECOMMENDED PAGE STRUCTURE
- **Section 1:** Compact Contact Hero
- **Section 2:** Quick Contact Action Dock
- **Section 3:** Site Visit Request
- **Section 4:** WhatsApp Enquiry Workflow
- **Section 5:** Location and Map
- **Section 6:** Detailed Project Enquiry Form
- **Section 7:** Service Area and Availability
- **Section 8:** FAQs
- **Section 9:** Final Contact Reassurance Panel

## 4. TONAL RHYTHM
Use:
- dark graphite hero
- soft concrete-grey action section
- dark or mid-graphite map section
- soft grey form section
- darker final reassurance panel

Do not use pure white.
Do not use continuous pure black.

## 5. IMAGE REQUIREMENTS
Define suitable image positions for:
- **compact hero background:** subtle atmospheric architectural lines or a blueprint overlay.
- **site-visit consultation visual:** an engineer or team reviewing plans on-site.
- **Mumbai/Nallasopara map or local construction visual:** an abstract map grid, coordinate visual, or regional development image.
- **final atmospheric contact background:** a high-quality structural steel frame or twilight construction site.

## 6. INTERACTION PERSONALITY
Use:
- contact-card hover feedback
- form focus states
- enquiry progress indicators
- animated map pin
- FAQ accordion
- success/error feedback
- subtle button confirmations

Do not use dramatic cinematic parallax or About-page blueprint step animations.

## 7. MOBILE EXPERIENCE
Define:
- sticky mobile contact action bar if appropriate
- tap-friendly buttons
- compact form layout
- readable map section
- no overlapping floating elements
- no duplicate WhatsApp buttons

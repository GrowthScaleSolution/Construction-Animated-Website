# Page Identity Matrix

## HOME
- **Purpose:** First impression and lead generation
- **Personality:** Cinematic and immersive
- **Motion:** Video depth, controlled parallax, powerful reveals
- **Rule:** Locked

## ABOUT
- **Purpose:** Company story and execution philosophy
- **Personality:** Editorial, architectural and disciplined
- **Motion:** Blueprint lines, image masks, measured reveals

## SERVICES
- **Purpose:** Explain capabilities and generate service enquiries
- **Personality:** Technical, organised and capability-focused
- **Suggested layouts:** service explorer, expandable technical panels, capability sections
- **Motion:** technical panel reveals, measurement lines, expandable details

## PROJECT GALLERY
- **Purpose:** Build trust through project visuals
- **Personality:** Visual, immersive and portfolio-focused
- **Suggested layouts:** masonry gallery, featured project, before/during/after, project lightbox
- **Motion:** image masks, gallery transitions, project expansion

## CONTACT / SITE VISIT
- **Purpose:** Convert users into WhatsApp, call and site-visit enquiries
- **Personality:** Direct, local and action-focused
- **Suggested layouts:** map-led layout, enquiry workflow, contact dashboard, site-visit form
- **Motion:** map pin, form interactions, enquiry progress and FAQ accordion
